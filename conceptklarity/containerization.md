In this assignment, you are required to demonstrate a clear understanding of Docker’s internal architecture, specifically how images, layers, and containers function and interact. The focus is on conceptual reasoning about Docker behavior rather than writing Dockerfiles or executing complex commands. This assignment evaluates your ability to explain Docker performance characteristics and lifecycle behavior in a project context.

You must make a meaningful Docker architecture–related contribution to your project repository. This may include documentation, architectural notes, diagrams, or structured explanations that describe how Docker images are built from layers, how containers are created from images, and how these concepts influence build performance, image size, and runtime behavior. The contribution must be submitted as a publicly accessible pull request.

In addition to the PR, you must record a screen-share video demo and upload it to Google Drive with access set to “anyone with the link can view.” In the video, you must walk through your PR contribution and explain Docker images, layers, and containers in your own words, referencing your project where relevant.
